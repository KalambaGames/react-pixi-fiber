The PixiJS 4 to 7 adapters create the filter tags `AlphaFilter`, `BlurFilter`, `ColorMatrixFilter`, `DisplacementFilter` and `NoiseFilter`, and add the `FXAAFilter` tag, imported from the adapter package. The PixiJS 5 and 6 adapters release a filter without a call to `destroy`.
